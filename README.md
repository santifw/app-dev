# My Favorite Movie: Coraline 🧵

## 🎬 About the Movie

**Coraline** is a *dark fantasy and horror animated movie* released in **2009**, directed by Henry Selick and based on the novel written by Neil Gaiman.

The story follows a young girl named Coraline Jones who discovers a mysterious door in her new home. Behind that door is an alternate world where everything seems perfect. However, she soon discovers that the other world hides something terrifying.
